RoastMath

Honest coffee roasting math. What the beans lose, when to drop the batch, what a scoop actually holds, and when the bag is done.

The shrinkage tax. Light roasts lose 14% of their weight, dark 19%. Buy green by the roasted weight you want, and see the real cost per roasted kilo - not the number on the green price tag.
The crack starts the clock. Give it your first-crack time and a development-ratio target; it hands back the exact drop time. 18-25% is where sweetness lives; under 15% is grass, over 30% is cardboard.
Scoops lie, grams don't. Caffeine per gram barely changes with roast level - but dark beans are less dense, so a scoop of dark delivers less coffee and less caffeine. Rest windows, peak days, and the stale cliff included.
